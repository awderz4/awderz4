I'm a **full-stack engineer** who likes turning messy real-world problems into clean, maintainable systems.
I work across **two full stacks** (TypeScript and Python), ship **Flutter** apps to production, and care a lot about architecture, performance and developer experience.

## 🚀 Highlights from my current role

Some of the larger pieces I've designed and shipped on a production e-commerce backend:

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**🔔 Notification service**
Event-driven customer notifications across email and other channels, including abandoned-cart reminders and lifecycle messages, scheduled as background jobs.

**🔎 Search index integrity**
Self-healing sync between the database and the search engine: content fingerprint validation, on-demand checks, health checks and automatic recovery after repeated failures.

**🧭 Approval workflow engine**
Configurable maker-checker approvals driven by uploaded BPMN diagrams, with element-level validation and a safe expression parser for workflow conditions.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**📈 Observability pipeline**
Structured request and background job logging into dashboards, with credential redaction, payload limits and fine-grained, settings-driven control.

**⚡ Storefront performance**
Cached page aggregation and navigation trees, repository-pattern rewrites and SQL-level pricing optimizations for high-traffic endpoints.

**📦 Bulk catalog tools**
Robust product import/export with row-level validation and downloadable error reports, plus a filter-based discount engine for large catalogs.
